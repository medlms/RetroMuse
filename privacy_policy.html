Privacy Policy for RetroMuse Music Player

Last updated: August 3, 2026

RetroMuse Music Player ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and permissions.

1. Information Collection and Use

RetroMuse Music Player is designed as an offline-first local audio player. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to external servers.

2. Device Permissions

To provide offline music playback and library organization, the application requests the following permissions:

  • Storage & Audio Access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used exclusively to scan, read, and organize audio files stored locally on your device. Your audio files never leave your device.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Used to keep music playing in the background when you exit the app or lock your device screen.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to render media playback controls and album artwork in your device's notification shade and lock screen widget.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use certain device information (such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and system diagnostic data) to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our app, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security

Because RetroMuse Music Player operates locally on your device, your music library, playlists, custom equalizer presets, and favorites remain stored securely on your local device storage.

5. Children's Privacy

RetroMuse Music Player does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
